This week's release brings global rankings, dungeon runs that survive a disconnect, the Green Knight's Hallow's Eve arena and a long list of fixes. Most of the fixes target crashes players hit on the live server over the last few days.

## New

- **Global rankings.** A new rankings page shows every dungeon's board, updated live. Open it from the new **Rankings** button on the HUD, or press **View Ranks** on the level-complete plate to jump straight to that dungeon's board. Dread runs have their own boards, and only players who actually ran a dungeon are ranked on it.
- **Dungeon runs survive a disconnect.** If you refresh the page, your game crashes or the server restarts for maintenance mid-run, logging back in puts you back in the same run. Your progress is kept and the enemies you killed stay dead. Boss intro cutscenes play again, and a half-damaged boss starts back at full health.
- **Hallow's Eve comes to the square.** The Green Knight's arena is in, and all of Black Rose Mire sits under his night sky. Look for lantern skulls with green fire in their eyes, candlelit windows and a spinning green portal.
- **A voice for repeat deaths.** From your fourth death in the same boss room on, each new death plays one of four "Serdar" clips. Party members in the room hear it too. Type `/serdar` to turn it off for every character on your account.

## Balance

- **Dread dungeons** give 30% more base XP and gold. Their enemies' base health multiplier is now **2.5×**, and party health scaling counts everyone who entered the dungeon.
- **Rune swaps** on four items:
  - Erug, Cover of Ku Sang: Resist Earth → Critical Power
  - Resolution of the Subworld: Main Stat (Balance) → Attack
  - King Mehtep's Royal Burial Seal: Max HP → Critical Chance
  - Dino's Helm of Hot Hymns: Main Stat (Exp) → Attack, Life Damage → Critical Power
- **Loot.** A full dungeon clear pays the full Dragon Ore again, and bosses can now drop gear above the run's gear cap. Legendary gear can drop even when an item has no rare version, and dungeon rewards use your character's current bonuses.

## Fixes

### Crashes and the screen

- Fixed several crashes in live games: in animations, in level art and in floor collision.
- Fixed the game crashing when a hatched egg released its pet, and when the window was resized.
- Fixed a frame-rate drop when the Green Knight takes his Sentinel form.
- The town background no longer smears into streaks or turns grey when the clock goes from Evening to Night.
- Damage numbers sit just above enemies again instead of floating far overhead.

### Combat and dungeons

- A party member's damage-over-time effects hit enemies again, whoever's game controls those enemies.
- Royal Intervention's fire spirits die in one hit again.
- Hiding Out's revived twin puts up a real fight again.
- Dread dungeons wait for the boss to die and its defeat scene to end before showing the rank plate, and Dread West Wing shares every enemy with the whole party.
- An enemy's loot no longer drops above its own floor, and a door no longer opens from the ledge above it.
- A power that ends early releases your character from its casting pose.

### Home, town and mounts

- Leaving your house puts you back where you were standing, not at the start of the map. A jump no longer sends Home or "go to" back to the entrance either.
- A mount left to graze or rest stays on solid ground instead of falling through the map, and keeps its own head while it does.
- Closing an old connection no longer rolls your character back.

### Pets and guilds

- Opening a chest that contains a pet no longer stops the egg that is hatching.
- A swapped pet no longer shows, or counts, in two slots.
- Guild officers can change the ranks below them.
- The Treasure Trove panel shows the sigils it paid out.

### Playing in the browser

- Every address opens the Adobe Flash Player page. The browser version (Ruffle) now lives at **/web**.
- If Ruffle runs out of memory, the page reloads instead of freezing, and Ruffle no longer stops with a "cannot recursively acquire mutex" error.
- Refreshing the page now ends your old session at once, so **Enter Game** works on the first click.

## Behind the scenes

The server now logs its memory use every ten minutes and has twice as much memory available. Together these should keep long sessions stable while we track down a slow memory leak.
