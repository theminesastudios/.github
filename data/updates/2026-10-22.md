This release puts Dungeon Blitz R on your phone and tablet, brings Bezix the donkey and a mailbox for your keep, sets fixed daily Dragon Ore payouts and fixes a long list of crashes and boss-fight bugs.

## Play on your phone or tablet

You can now play on **/web** with touch controls.

- A movement stick, plus **Attack** and **Power** buttons. Tap to use them, or drag to aim.
- Skills 1–6 sit on two rings around Attack. Tap to cast at the nearest enemy, or drag to aim and let go to cast. Let go on the X to cancel.
- **Mount**, **Pet**, **Use**, **Map** and **Bag** buttons, plus a menu with Spellbook, Talents, Social, Store, Chat and fullscreen.
- Settings for auto aim, left-handed layout, vibration, button size and opacity. You can also move the button groups anywhere.
- Install /web as a full-screen app from the touch menu, or add it to your iOS home screen.
- Aiming shows the game's own cursor, so where you aim is where your attack goes.

## New

- **Bezix the donkey.** A new mount that rides 20% faster. Redeem it with `/code:aiai` for every character on your account. Each mount's speed now shows in its tooltip.
- **A mailbox for your keep.** Read the latest news and send player reports from the mailbox in front of your keep.
- **Guild officers** can now accept or decline join requests and edit the guild description and banner.
- **Sentinel: Electrocute.** A new damage over time that stacks up to 10, applied by Holy Smash, Shockwave and Defiance. Unstable Barrier's shield now shows in your health orb.
- **Death sound.** When you enter a dungeon, chat reminds you that `/serdar` turns the death sound off.
- **Chat.** An English profanity filter, and lines can be up to 2,000 characters. Only the All tab is sent to Discord now.

## Balance and economy

- **Green Knight.** You must be level 30 or higher to challenge him.
- **Dragon Ore.** Set daily payouts. Normal dungeons pay 25/20/15/10/5 for your first five clears of the day, then 5. Dread pays 50/40/30/20/10, then 10. Runs under 20% clear don't use up a step. Material Find adds 25% ore for every +100%.
- **Potions** now run down with dungeon clear instead of time. One potion covers 200% clear, so two full runs. A message tells you how much is left after a run or when you unequip.
- **Mammoth Idol packs** cost a third of the Dragon Ore they used to.
- **Respec Stone:** 160 sigils, down from 320.
- **Gear.** Rune and stat changes on many mage, paladin and rogue items, plus Carapace Robe, Starsmite, Mudslinger, Eran and the Siren of Black Rose Mire.

## Fixes

### Crashes and the screen

- Fewer "Your game seems to have crashed" screens, including the crash when entering a level and many out-of-memory crashes in Flash.
- Monsters no longer turn invisible or freeze on one frame.
- Grey or blank backgrounds now redraw.

### Combat and dungeons

- Joining a party boss fight mid-fight no longer freezes mobs, hides the boss or replays the intro. You can't damage bosses during their intro anymore.
- Slapdash Decoy and other summons show once per cast, not twice.
- Nephit's portal eyes come back if you crash and rejoin the final fight, so the portals can be closed.
- Loot from flying enemies, like the Psychophages in Wither the Witch, lands where you can reach it.
- Boss rushes no longer score as a full clear.
- The Goblin Kidnappers cave no longer opens when you walk over the hill above it.

### Home, items and guilds

- Coffer keys and Treasure Trove keys are now separate, and the coffer board no longer shows up as 40 chests in your bag.
- Upgrading one discipline tower no longer looks like it's upgrading all three. You can now speed up or cancel an upgrade, and the right tower shows on your Home plot.
- Talent training can't go past the points your tower level unlocks.
- Charms put into gear you're wearing no longer disappear.
- Potion stacks over 100 now show the right count.
- `/gquit` now really takes you out of the guild.
- The mailbox **!** no longer follows you out of your home.

### Touch controls

- Skills 4–6 always cast when you tap them. The game decides about mana and cooldowns, and dimmed buttons are just a hint.
- Skills you rebound to other keys now work.
- Double-tap and pinch no longer zoom the page in Safari.
- Skill, mount and pet buttons show their real hotbar icons.

### Text

- Enemy lines name you instead of your summon, Ooyak's minion says "offspring", and the barracks dungeon is now called The Imperial Barracks.

## Behind the scenes

The server runs smoother with lots of players online. Finished dungeon runs are cleaned up in minutes instead of about 40, and the heaviest server tasks are faster.
