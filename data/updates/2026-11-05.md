## Dungeons
- **Ring of Fire:** the intro stand-in no longer counts as the boss, so runs stop ending at 44–97% of Davan's health. Davan spawns again. After a refresh or a late join, the boss room continues from your current stage instead of replaying the intro.
- **Blood and Sand:** the intro "Champion of the Sun" stand-in no longer counts as a boss kill.
- **Dream Within a Dream:** the dragon portal comes back every time the dragon calls it.
- **Green Knight:** spawns even if his showcase cutscene is cut short.
- **Unearthing the Past:** totems in the boss rooms come back when the room revives them.
- **Dread Goblin Hideout:** enemies are now shared, so the whole party fights the same goblins. Tag Ugo regains health at a set pace after a party wipe.
- **Castle Hocke:** Titus no longer appears twice by the Last Stand door.
- If you join a run already in progress, you no longer see enemies that respawned only on your screen.
- Leaving a dungeon no longer drops a helper into a region they haven't unlocked. They return to where they entered.

## Legends' Inn & Guild Raids
- The gate can be opened on any day, once a week per guild.
- An opened gate stays open for 24 hours, even past midnight. The lobby shows how much time is left, and guild chat gets a warning before it closes.
- Extra raid rooms cost 5 Mammoth Idols, and a guild can have up to 100 rooms (up from 4). Officers can open rooms too.
- Wounded enemies keep their health through a server restart.
- Side chests that aren't part of the stage no longer pay out, which closes a gold exploit.
- Staff can now disband a guild and send its members a notice explaining why.

## Gameplay & Balance
- **Heavy Blows** is now 5/10/15/17/20% (was 2/5/10/12/15%).
- **Cleaving Blows:** fixed the rune bonus, which never applied to trained ranks.
- **Call the Horde** and **Bolster the Horde** summon more ghouls at higher ranks.
- Rune swaps on many pieces of gear.
- Paladin Mystic set lights now pulse in your discipline colour.
- Neo's skill quests count every hotbar skill, not only skills with a cooldown.
- A health orb now revives a player who died in mid-air.
- The find bonus from lockbox pets in your active slot now applies to actual drops.
- **Scoring:** a 100% clear now counts as a full clear, and shots still in the air no longer count as misses.
- Skill upgrades take one minute, so the tutorial's "Speed Up" step shows again. Respec Stones take one minute to forge and are still free.
- /pick no longer works while you're dead, and it leaves health orbs on the floor for other players.

## UI & Client
- New option: **Hide health and mana numbers**.
- The friends-only party invite setting is now saved between sessions.
- Find stats in the Armory no longer show a tenth too low.
- Gear search now finds gear with Trove charms.
- Guild donation announcements no longer break guild chat.

## Touch / Mobile
- Tapping a touch button closes an open chat first, so skill and movement keys no longer get typed into chat.
- A new eye button brings back hidden touch controls.
- The movement stick stays in one place, attacks aim the way you last faced, and lifting your finger no longer swings the camera. The second attack button has been removed.
- Chrome on phones no longer shows the "access other apps" prompt.

## Security & Fair Play
- New protections against the bot floods from 29 October.
- Kills only pay out for enemies the server is tracking, and self-respawn healing can't go above your max HP.
- New checks help staff spot speed and god-mode cheats. For now they only flag players for review.
- The /lostpw page has been removed because it let anyone check which accounts are linked to Discord.
- The website now runs behind Cloudflare.

## Performance
- Smoother servers: enemy AI and player visibility now only check nearby areas, and enemies with no players nearby pause.
- Faster page loads thanks to better caching.
- More reliable restarts, with a short maintenance hold every time.
