## New: Dungeon Rifts

Dread runs can now roll **Rift affixes** that change how each dungeon plays:

- **Hazardous, Enraged, Molten, Toxic, Fortified and Frenzied.**
- **Toxic** poisons you every 4 seconds, and you don't regenerate out of combat while poisoned.
- **Molten** has a new lava warning, and the lava overlay fades out after each wave.
- A Rift never rolls Toxic and Molten together.

How it works:

- Rift mode is **opt-in per character**. The party leader decides for the group, and you can't change it while inside a dungeon.
- Open Rift details with the new **Rift button** on the news bar.
- The active Rift shows when you enter a dungeon.
- Rifts rotate **every Monday at 00:00 (GMT+3)**.
- The wiki has a new **Rifts** category with Dungeon Rifts and Rift Affixes pages.

## Combat & UI

- Damage-over-time numbers are coloured by type. Holy Fire is bright white-yellow and Bleeding is pinkish.
- Electrocute, Holy Fire and Bind have their own status icons.
- Your health orb turns poison-green in a Toxic Rift.
- New options: **Merge damage numbers** and **Show debuff stacks**. The Options list also has more room.
- The loading screen no longer flickers during transfers.

## Balance

- **Dread difficulty:** enemy HP is now ×3 instead of ×4.
- **Paladin, Cleaving Blows:** now has a 20-second cooldown, and its duration text is correct.
- **Firebrand and Cleaving Blows** now last a fixed time instead of scaling with Expertise.

## Neo's Quests & Shops

- Every dungeon kill now counts toward kill quests.
- The weekly bonus is bigger and now includes gold.
- Daily Neo quests take about half an hour of play.
- NPCs who are no longer in the story, including Skitts, no longer count for "Talk to 3 Townsfolk".
- The Stable Store and Sigil Shop now refresh weekly.

## Bug Fixes

- The Emerald Throne boss is now named "Paladin Lothyr".
- The Prodigal Son boss alternates its shields again.
- Rejoining a party no longer heals the boss.
- Sentinel Form's exit cooldown now depends on how much energy is left in the bar.
- Buffs from party members no longer drop off early.
- Party runs always open with the correct Rift.
- Rift settings now carry over when you move between areas, and Rifts no longer cause false deaths.
- Turning off touch controls on a phone no longer locks you out.
- Forge: you can no longer press Craft with no materials selected.
- Fixed a timer crash and several exploits.

## Security & Performance

- Stronger server-side protection against cheating.
- Linking Discord is now more secure.
- The hub runs much more smoothly when it's busy.
- The rankings page loads without slowing the game down.
- General save and performance improvements.

Thanks for playing, and good luck in the Rifts!
