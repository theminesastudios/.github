This release opens Legends' Inn as a weekly guild raid, brings Neo's daily and weekly quests, adds Dread gates to five more regions, makes Tome of Power upgrades instant and lets you teleport from the world map.

## Legends' Inn guild raid

Legends' Inn now opens from your guild leader's Guild Hall as a guild raid.

- The raid opens every **Wednesday** (GMT+3). Once the gate is open, it stays open for the whole day.
- Up to 10 fighters per room, level 30 and up. Fallen fighters rest for 7 minutes while the next in line takes their place.
- Enemies are shared by the whole room, and the party frames show every fighter in it.
- Each stage's portal opens once every enemy is down, with a counter for how many are left.
- Clearing at least 90% of a stage pays 70 Mammoth Idols, 5 Treasure Troves and 5 keys. Side chests give 20,000 gold each, once a day per chest.
- Guilds can now hold up to 60 players.
- Leaders and officers can remove a player with `raidkick: <name>` and let them back with `raidunkick: <name>`. Fighters who stand still for 10 minutes are sent back to the Guild Hall.

## New

- **Neo's Quests.** Talk to Neo in your keep for five daily and two weekly quests, the same for everyone. Each quest pays its own reward, from Royal Sigils, XP, gold and Dragon Ore to Treasure Troves, a Dragon Key and charms. Finish a whole set for 25 Mammoth Idols.
- **Dread gates.** Wolf's End, Black Rose Mire, Cemetery Hills, Castle Hocke and Stormshard Mountains now have their own gate to Dread, marked on the world map.
- **Map teleport.** Pick a region on the world map and teleport there for 15 Silver Sigils. This replaces the `/teleport` command.
- **Instant skill upgrades.** Tome of Power skill ranks land as soon as you pay. Only buildings still take time.
- **Sentinel Form.** You can now cast Sentinel skills while in the form, and form attacks give mana back like basic attacks.
- **/pick.** In dungeons, `/pick` picks up every loot drop on screen, for drops that land out of reach.
- **Wiki.** Gear shows as it looks in the game when you hover it, runes are named by what they do, and a new Talent Calculator plans a build for any discipline.

## Balance

- **Talentstones.** A retune across poison, bleed and fire stones. Sentinel gets Divine Lightning and Lightning Expansion for Electrocute. Cursed Sword, Cursed Armor, Fervor and Conviction were reworked.
- **Shadowstalker.** Shadow Legion summons three clones again, Heart Seeker's damage is back, and a kill now makes you invisible for 5 seconds.
- **Sentinel.** The passive gives 0.15% of Max HP as Attack and Expertise. Sentinel Form's second blow applies Electrocute.
- **Templar.** Divine Word applies Holy Fire from rank 1, and Celestial Lance hits a small area.
- **Charms.** Amethyst and Ruby crit bonuses are halved.
- **Gear.** Rune swaps on 61 more items across every rarity.
- **Mammoth Idols** you earn from now on stay on the character that earned them.

## Fixes

### Combat and dungeons

- Frozen or stunned enemies stop attacking as well as moving.
- Chaos Wave stacks no longer drop enemy damage to zero.
- Gear runes' damage bonus now applies to your hits.
- Rushed runs only count as a full clear when you clear the whole dungeon.
- Party loot lands on the floor nearest the kill, not in a room above.
- Felbridge's west door takes you back to the swamp when Arachnae's run isn't open yet.

### Home, guilds and the screen

- Coffers open again.
- The whole world map draws on scaled and high-DPI windows.
- Leaving a guild no longer freezes the game.
- Promoting or demoting guild members is much faster.

### Touch controls

- A held attack now follows the direction you drag.
- Buttons no longer get stuck, input is smoother, and controls stay clear of the notch and the Discord overlay.

## Behind the scenes

The game now runs in the launcher's Adobe AIR engine. Guild rosters load from memory, which cut a large share of the server's CPU use, and in-game chat is no longer posted to the Discord channel by default.
