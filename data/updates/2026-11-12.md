# **Talent Builds**
You no longer have to respec every time you change playstyle. Your talent window now has **3 build slots**, for example a bleed build and a crit build saved side by side.
- Slot 1 is free. Slot 2 costs **1,000,000 gold** and Slot 3 costs **100 Mammoth Idols** (bought once per character)
- Each discipline keeps its own builds
- Swapping is **free** and works anywhere you can open the talent screen
- Click a build's name to rename it
- **Load** takes a Talent Calculator link from the wiki and puts that build on your tree. Nothing changes until you press **Apply**
