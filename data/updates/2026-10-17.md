A smaller release on the heels of yesterday's: Ruby charms give twice the Critical Bonus, the stable gets new prices, The Minesa Studios gets its own guild banner, and a batch of fixes, starting with one that kept the game from loading at all.

## New

- **Ruby charms, doubled.** Every rank of Ruby now grants **+1% Critical Bonus**, up to **+10%** on Infinite Ruby (it was 0.5% per rank, capped at 5%). A forged Critical Bonus secondary rises with it.
- **New prices at the stable.** Mounts and pets are repriced, and the Dragon Ore from a dungeon now falls each time you repeat it in the same day.
- **A banner for the studio.** The Minesa Studios guild flies the studio's logo as its emblem. It's reserved: no other guild can pick it.
- **Minesa all over the title screens.** The loading screen, the login sword and the about box carry the Dungeon Blitz R logo, with The Minesa Studios beside Blue Mammoth Games. The link bar's buttons now open our Discord, and **Like** is now **Join**.

## Fixes

### Getting into the game

- Fixed the game stopping at a blank grey screen on start-up, in both Flash and the browser version.
- Logging in no longer fails for a character at the level cap, or when the connection drops halfway through.
- The game no longer crashes when the screen can't get enough memory to draw.

### On screen

- Gaps in the level art no longer show a frozen picture of an earlier moment.
- Level art lost to low memory now repairs itself once memory frees up, instead of spreading as you walk.
- Shadow Clones look like ghosts on every player's screen, not only the caster's.

### Combat and loot

- Leaving a dungeon and walking back in, or rejoining after a logout or restart, no longer pays out its dead enemies a second time, and rejoining no longer shows them dying again.
- A party member's damage-over-time effects hit enemies again.
