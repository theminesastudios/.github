Dungeon Blitz, back again

Dungeon Blitz was a free-to-play browser brawler: you picked a Paladin, Rogue or Mage, chose one of three disciplines and fought through dungeons with friends. Dungeon Blitz R brings it back, playable in your browser or with a launcher, with multiplayer, fixes and balancing.

What is Dungeon Blitz R?

Dungeon Blitz R is a free-to-play, open-source revival of Dungeon Blitz, the side-scrolling brawler with light RPG and MMO elements. The Minesa Studios rebuilt it with multiplayer, fixes and balancing, so you can play Dungeon Blitz again.

Can I still play Dungeon Blitz?

Yes. Dungeon Blitz R is out now, released publicly on October 30, 2026. Play it in your browser at dungeonblitzr.theminesa.studio/web, or download the launcher for Windows or macOS.

Is Dungeon Blitz R free?

Yes, it's free to play.

Do I need Flash Player to play?

No plugin needed. The browser version runs on Ruffle, a Flash Player emulator, and the launcher is a standalone app for Windows and macOS.

Which classes and disciplines can I play?

Three classes with three disciplines each: the Paladin (Sentinel, Justicar, Templar), the Rogue (Viperblade, Shadowstalker, Soulthief) and the Mage (Flameseer, Frostbringer, Necromancer).

Who makes Dungeon Blitz R?

The Minesa Studios, a small team of players. Dungeon Blitz was originally created by Blue Mammoth Games. Dungeon Blitz R is a fan revival, open source on GitHub, and the community lives on our Discord. Patch notes are on the Updates page.
